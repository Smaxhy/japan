      Things to do

      A loose menu. December extras like markets, lights and the New Year's Eve bells are flagged with their dates.

      Ride

      Loops that start at your door. Tokyo's east side is flat and the river paths are wide, so it's great biking. December mornings are cold and clear, and it's dark by about 4:30pm.

      Getting a bike

      Rules locals actually follow

      Ride on the left with traffic. Sidewalks only where a blue sign allows bikes, and pedestrians win.

      Lights on after dark, which is 4:30pm in December. Police do stop foreigners for this.

      No phone, no umbrella, no earbuds while riding. Fines got real in 2024.

      Park only in racks or share ports. Bikes left on the street get towed, and getting one back costs about ¥3,000–5,000.

      Helmets are officially "make an effort". Share bikes don't come with one.

      Drinking and riding is treated like drink driving. Walk the bike home from the izakaya.

      Day trips

      Leave early: the sun sets around 4:30pm. Asakusa Station, 8 minutes' walk from you, is the Tobu line terminal, so Nikko and Ashikaga leave from your doorstep.
